The question

How does Sydney's seasonal climate pattern, shown through its climate normal and station network, compare with broader regional patterns in Australia?

The data

I used Esri's World Historical Climate – Monthly Averages for GHCN-D Stations for 1981–2010, a global climate layer based on NOAA NCEI's Global Historical Climatology Network-Daily (GHCN-Daily), Version 3.24. The dataset contains monthly mean temperature and precipitation data for the 1981–2010 period. Esri is the publisher, while the underlying station and climate data come from NOAA NCEI's GHCN-Daily dataset. The layer is licensed under the Esri Master License Agreement. The attribute table contains 7,480 records, although the older layer description refers to a smaller station count; this discrepancy reflects the layer's revision history and is an important data-provenance issue.
